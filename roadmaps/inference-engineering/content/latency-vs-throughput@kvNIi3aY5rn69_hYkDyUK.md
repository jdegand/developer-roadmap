# Latency vs. Throughput
 
Lower latency means faster responses per user; higher throughput means more total requests served per unit time. These goals are often in tension. Increasing batch size improves throughput but worsens per-user latency. Choosing the right balance is one of the primary decisions in configuring an inference service.
