# Model Selection
 
The most important decision in model performance optimization is which model to use. Smaller models with fewer parameters are faster and cheaper to serve than larger ones. The goal is to find the smallest model that meets quality requirements for the task. Model architecture also determines which inference engines and optimization techniques are available, so popular architectures with broad tooling support are preferable.
