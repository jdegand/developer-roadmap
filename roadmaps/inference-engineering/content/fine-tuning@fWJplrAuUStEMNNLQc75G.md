# Fine-Tuning
 
Fine-tuning takes a pretrained foundation model and adapts it to a specific domain by training on additional labeled data. A small fine-tuned model can match a much larger general model on constrained tasks like text-to-SQL. Smaller models are faster and cheaper to serve, so fine-tuning is one of the highest-leverage tools for hitting latency and cost targets.
